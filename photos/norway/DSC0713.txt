...There was one day when I started from high up on this mountain, and I did a loop that was suggested to me around this lake near the top of the mountain. It was beautiful. After crossing around to a steeper side of a mountain, I look up, and I decide I want to go higher. So I go off trail for an hour or so. It's steep, the snow is tough to work through... After an hour or so the mountain completely disappears into white. I realize a storm is coming. The storm doesn't appear to be too bad, so I keep going. Eventually it gets to me. The wind is intense, I can't see very far in front of me because the snow coming at me is so thick...
