...I skied for a few hours up to the top of this mountain, only to see that the mountain indeed continues. For these hours there is no sign of civilization around - no buildings or houses or anything remotely resembling infrastructure (with the exception of the cross country ski tracks, but these are being blown over as well). Then, all of the sudden, a small collection of what I can only understand to be homes appears. Old, old homes. Roofs are just moss. They're small, more like shacks. But I imagine them to be cozy on the inside. Not more than 10 of them. I actually witness a man leaving one of them. He was very old. Ancient. He had skis and poles that matched his ancient qualities. He was remarkably slow. It reminds me of Professor S's slowness. But I get the sense that S is slow despite his desire to get things done. He's slow and it clashes with the environment around him. But the old man on the mountain was not slow compared to his environment. He was just the right speed. I actually make eye contact with the man, and once he looks away he doesn't look back to me...
